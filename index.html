Executive Viability Summary

Viability Strengths

  • High coverage in mature levers (technology, knowledge).
  • Strong commitment from key stakeholders (UNEP, CCAC, IAWP, GMH, AVINA, GAIA).
  • Alignment with global goals (SDG 12/13, GST).
  • Scalability potential with replicable models.

Critical Gaps and Risks

  • Dependence on the short term (70% of actions in 2025).
  • Low maturity in Finance and Policy (~40% of actions).
  • Social inclusion is aspirational but under-executed.
  • "Management/Disposal" category is very weak.
  • Lack of a unified dashboard for KPI monitoring.
